End-to-end journey

How Blab reaches launch

The product journey is mostly working. The final gap sits between a polished learning chat, a Play-signed invite journey, and a complete release pass.

Working Needs manual check Needs finishing Blocked
  1. Working

    Account access

    Email, Google, reset, account editing, logout, and deletion.

  2. Working

    Create an invite

    Pick a learning language, create the link, share or copy it.

  3. Check

    Recipient joins

    Open the link, carry context through sign-up, claim one shared chat.

  4. Working

    Live conversation

    Realtime messages, replies, edits, reactions, media, read and typing states.

  5. Finish

    Learn in chat

    Normal and Practice modes, word lookup, translation, corrections, forms.

  6. Check

    Safety & recovery

    Report, block, offline queue, notifications, privacy controls, crash reports.

  7. Blocked

    Play-signed release

    Play App Signing, verified links, OAuth certificate, production bundle.

  8. Prepare

    Store package

    Legal copy, Data Safety, screenshots, feature graphic, reviewer access.

  9. Pending

    Closed test

    Internal release, eligible tester requirement, crash and feedback pass.

  10. Pending

    Production

    Repeat the full audit on the Play build, then make the final go/no-go call.
