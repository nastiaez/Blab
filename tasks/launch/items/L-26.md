Status: `Not started`

## Finding confirmation

On Bob's Android emulator, a photo with an English caption first rendered as a completed German translation. After disabling networking, force-stopping Blab, and reopening the same cached chat, the photo and authored caption remained available but the completed German view did not. Reconnecting restored the server-cached translation.

## Scope

- Persist completed viewer-specific message and photo-caption translations in the existing account-scoped device recovery cache.
- Restore the exact completed learning-language variant after an offline restart on the same device.
- Keep variants isolated by account, chat, message, target language, primary known language, source version, and private language revision.
- Reject stale cached output after a message edit or language-revision change.

## Non-goals

- Reinstall or new-device recovery in V1.
- OCR or translation of text drawn inside a photo.
- Changing provider-side retry or grammatical-form audit policy.
