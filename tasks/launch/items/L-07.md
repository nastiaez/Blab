## Finding confirmation

Current behavior:

## Decision record

Required decisions:

1. What exact legal name and contact location should identify the individual data controller/operator publicly?
2. Should Blab adopt the proposed solo-operator moderation and deletion service levels below?
3. Which monitored email address should receive deletion, safety, and moderation requests?

Options and tradeoffs:

### Operator identity

- **A (recommended):** publish the owner's exact legal name plus a valid public service/postal address. This gives the clearest controller identity but makes the address public. A professional service address may be used only if it is legally valid for the owner.
- **B:** publish the exact legal name and only `Berlin, Germany`. This exposes less personal information but may not satisfy all controller/contact or German website-notice obligations; legal review would remain a launch risk.
- Do not publish the Play developer display name or a brand as though it were a legal entity unless it is the actual registered operator.

### External deletion path

- **A (recommended):** add a dedicated `/delete-account.html` resource with a prominent email request action. Requests must come from the account email where possible; the operator verifies account control, never asks for a password, completes deletion within 7 calendar days, and confirms completion. The in-app path remains immediate.
- **B:** build authenticated web self-service deletion. This is smoother but requires a web auth and session flow that is disproportionate to the current static website and increases security scope.

### Moderation operation

- **A (recommended for the closed MVP):** use an authenticated report RPC that validates chat/message relationships and captures a minimal evidence snapshot; maintain a structured report queue and append-only action log in Supabase; review the queue every day; triage credible child-safety or imminent-harm reports within 24 hours and all other reports within 72 hours; suspend accounts and restrict writes through database authorization; remove content/accounts where required; preserve report evidence for a documented limited period; log decisions; and use the public safety email as the urgent fallback. Supabase Studio is the operator console for this MVP.
- **B:** build a separate moderation dashboard and notification service now. This improves operator ergonomics but adds authentication, deployment, notification-provider, and admin-security scope before the first closed release.
- **C:** remove messaging/UGC from launch. This avoids moderation operation but removes the core product and requires broad product/store changes.

Approved decision: **Option A**, including:

- Create the missing public deletion endpoint and request path.
- Keep immediate authenticated deletion in the app.
- Use Supabase Studio as the closed-MVP moderation console.
- Review the moderation queue daily.
- Triage child-safety or imminent-harm reports within 24 hours and other reports within 72 hours.
- Retain closed report evidence for 180 days unless a legal hold requires longer.
- Enforce operator suspensions at database authorization boundaries and audit moderation decisions and reversals.
