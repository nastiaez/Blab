The decisions hiding inside this

Your own words never disappear
Both keep your English on screen the whole time you're waiting. You wrote it, you should see it — nothing goes blank, nothing gets replaced by a spinner.
One lane, start to finish
No second line under the bubble at any point. What you're watching is the same single line changing language.
Strictly one thing at a time
The English is completely gone before the bubble starts changing shape, and the bubble has completely stopped before the first German word arrives. A bubble in motion drags whatever is inside it, so the answer is to have nothing inside it while it moves.
Their messages don't do any of this
A message from the other person only appears once its translation is ready, so there is no gap to fill and no animation at all — it simply lands, already in your learning language. If the translation fails it lands in the original language rather than never arriving.
The two languages never share the same pixels
Two texts at half opacity in one spot smear into each other, worst at the opening words where both languages start at the same point. The wave avoids it in time: the original clears a spot before the translation reaches it.
The first 350ms stay still
Toggle it off and set the wait to 0.4s to see why — when Blab answers instantly, an animation that starts and stops immediately reads as a glitch. Holding still for a third of a second means fast replies just appear.
The bubble knows its final size before it moves
The German line is measured, hidden, in a real copy of the bubble before anything animates, so the bubble travels straight to its final shape — width and height together, as one motion — instead of resizing again once the text lands. Try shorter as well as longer; both directions are one move.
If it fails, it stays quiet
The band stops and your English simply stays. No error, no retry button — Blab keeps trying in the background and the line turns to German whenever it succeeds.

Still open: what Blab does with a message that has nothing to translate — laughter, emoji, a time, a name, a keyboard mash. Sound words get respelled in the learning language; everything else stays exactly as typed.
